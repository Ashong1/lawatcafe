5. Answered during the evaluation (no change needed)

  • Void approval: kept as an owner-approval queue by design, since the shop has one staff member per shift and the owner may approve remotely.
  • Security of the server on the guest Wi-Fi: guest devices are limited by firewall rules to internet access only; SSH and the admin pages of the server, firewall and DNS are blocked for them.
  • HTTPS, offline use, AI token limits, scheduled jobs, BIR receipts, Senior/PWD discounts and reprinting: already in place and explained.
